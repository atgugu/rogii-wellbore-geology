# Findings, with provenance

Every number in the README appears here with where it came from.

- **Reproduced** — regenerated by a script in this repository from the competition data; the output is in `results/`.
- **Recorded** — measured during the competition (July–August 2026) with code that depends on third-party artifacts or
  on a long pipeline that is not included. Reported as recorded, not re-run for this repository.

The competition: [ROGII Wellbore Geology Prediction](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rogii-wellbore-geology-prediction),
a code competition (9-hour limit, no internet), scored by pooled RMSE on `TVT` over every evaluation row of about 200 hidden wells.
The public board scored 26% of the hidden wells (about 50).

## 2. The identity and the dip sequence

**Identity (reproduced, `scripts/04_verify_identity.py`).** For each marker column
`ANCC, ASTNU, ASTNL, EGFDU, EGFDL, BUDA`, the within-well standard deviation of `TVT + Z − marker` has a median of
0.0065 ft and a maximum of 0.0089 ft over 766–773 wells; the spacing between adjacent markers has a within-well
standard deviation of at most 0.005 ft. Consequently only one surface needs to be modelled, and
`U = TVT + Z` is that surface up to a per-well constant.

**Dip form.** Anchored on the last known row `a`,

    TVT_i = TVT_a − (Z_i − Z_a) + Σ_{j ≤ i} dip_j · ΔMD_j

The first two terms are known at inference (the trajectory is given in full). Carrying `TVT` constant is the special
case in which `dU/dMD = dZ/dMD`; the fitted `U = U_a + λ (Z − Z_a)` has a pooled-optimal `λ = 0.982`, with per-well `λ`
having sd 0.20 (reproduced, `scripts/05_steering_lag.py`).

**Oracle ladders (reproduced).** Pooled RMSE over 773 wells; the dip rungs pin the level to the last known row.

| rung | RMSE (ft) | script |
|---|---|---|
| carry last TVT | 15.910 | 02, 03 |
| best constant / line / quadratic in TVT | 9.035 / 6.697 / 5.342 | 02 |
| smooth low-frequency (1201 ft moving average) | 3.036 | 02 |
| 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 dip segments | 7.587 / 4.323 / 3.024 / 2.252 | 03 |
| 6 / 8 / 12 / 16 | 1.573 / 1.211 / 0.811 / 0.605 | 03 |

The TVT-space rungs match the figures other competitors published (15.91 / 9.04 / 6.70 / 5.34), which is how the
evaluation mask was validated. Note that the dip rungs are *oracle* values: the segments are fitted to the truth.

## 3. Error anatomy (recorded)

On an out-of-fold reconstruction of the deployed pipeline (773 wells; the third-party learned track replaced
by a grouped out-of-fold retrain, since the shipped one is fitted on all training wells):

- RMSE versus measured-depth distance `d` (ft) from the anchor follows `√(2.002² + (0.0513 · d^0.654)²)`.
- The decomposition of squared error into level / linear trend / residual is 0.621 / 0.194 / 0.185, versus the exact
  values for a Brownian walk of 2/3, 1/5, 2/15. The "level" error is walk accumulation, not a per-well offset;
  removing the entire anchor nugget would be worth only 0.15 ft.
- 99.1% of the error energy is at wavelengths longer than 500 ft: there is no short-wavelength texture to recover.
- **Smoothing in `U`, not `TVT`.** The formation surface `U` is smooth while the well wiggles in `Z`, so the true
  `TVT` carries an anti-correlated wiggle (`dTVT ≈ −dZ`). Smoothing `TVT` removes it; smoothing `U = TVT + Z`
  keeps it. On a deliberately noisy base the gain was 0.22 ft for `U`-smoothing against 0.06 ft for
  `TVT`-smoothing. On the final, already-smooth base the gain was about 0.02 ft offline. The window was chosen as
  the bottom of an empirical bowl on out-of-fold data (a few hundred rows up to 901; 901 was shipped). A moving average beat a Savitzky–Golay filter and an L1 trend filter.

## 4. Gamma ray

| claim | value | status |
|---|---|---|
| a flexible U-space dynamic program fits GR better than the truth | on 72% of wells (mean emission cost 0.340 vs 0.410) | recorded |
| …and scores | 15.83 vs 13.91 carrying the last TVT (140 held-out wells) | recorded |
| truth beats same-complexity impostors under the GR likelihood, K=1 / 2 / 4 | 83.3 / 87.0 / 93.3% (120 wells) | reproduced (`scripts/07`) |
| …against impostors drawn tightly around the truth's slope, K=1 / 2 | 68.4 / 68.0% | recorded |
| total-variation dip DP | 0.25 ft on a synthetic two-segment well; 30.3 on real wells | recorded |
| label-free GR ranking of near-equal candidate tracks | 61–63% pairwise accuracy (400 wells) | recorded |

The first row explains why the second row happens, and the third why a re-ranking test was worth running. A
flexible search has too many degrees of freedom for the likelihood to be at the truth; a constrained search can rank
a handful of candidates; but the gain from ranking is small when the candidates are close and unavailable when they
are not.

## 5. Cross-well and steering signals

- **Hold-out geometry (reproduced, `scripts/06`).** At 1000 ft linkage radius, 86.8% of wells share a pad with
  another well. Dropping a random 20% of wells leaves the nearest remaining well a median 411 ft away
  (93.5% within 1,500 ft); dropping whole pads, 645 ft (85.8%); dropping a spatial block, 31,097 ft (0%).
- **Steering lag (reproduced, `scripts/05`).** The correlation between the 301 ft smoothed `dU/dMD` and `dZ/dMD`
  is 0.237 at zero lag and peaks at 0.241 when the trajectory is read 150 ft ahead.
- **Neighbour correction (recorded).** A distance-gated correction of `U` from same-family neighbours (gate 600 ft)
  gave −0.12 ft offline and about −0.07 ft on the board. This was the one cross-well operator that transferred
  at a useful fraction.
